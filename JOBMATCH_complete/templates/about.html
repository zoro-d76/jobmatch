Meet the Founder

Emanuel Owino Okumu

My name is Emanuel Owino Okumu. I am an aspiring software developer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I am currently studying at Computer Pride, where I am developing my knowledge and skills in technology.

I am passionate about software development, innovation and using technology to solve real-world problems. I believe that technology can create opportunities and make people's lives easier.

My Journey

JOBMATCH is a project built from my interest in technology and my desire to create something useful. I wanted to develop a platform that connects job seekers with employers in a simple and accessible way.

Building JOBMATCH is part of my learning journey as a software developer. It represents my creativity, determination and ambition to grow in the technology industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make job searching easier by connecting talented job seekers with employers. We aim to make employment opportunities easier to discover and access.

My Vision

My vision is to become a skilled software developer and create innovative digital solutions that make a positive difference in society.

Created with ambition and innovation.
