He is the founder and creator of JOBMATCH, a digital platform designed to connect job seekers with employers through technology.

His vision is to use software development and innovation to make job opportunities easier to discover and access.

Through JOBMATCH, Emmanuel aims to demonstrate how technology can help solve real-world challenges and create opportunities for people.
